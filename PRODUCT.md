# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Habitat and fire-safety engineers, NASA Space Apps judges, and space-curious visitors. They come to see what real NASA microgravity fire tests cover for a proposed Moon or Mars cabin atmosphere, and what was never tested. Judges see it in a three-minute demo; engineers stay longer in the Habitat explorer, the investigation table and the Q&A.

## Product Purpose
FireSight reads 20 NASA combustion investigations and 772 real test runs (PSI tables plus published Saffire IV–VI results). For any cabin atmosphere it shows coverage per fuel category, the nearest real runs with their sources, a fire walkthrough, and what crews did to put flames out. Success: a visitor trusts every number because each one leads back to a NASA source, and understands in seconds where the evidence stops.

## Brand Personality
Calm, precise, authoritative. It should feel like a NASA mission page or an Apple product page: one confident idea per screen, large clean type, real imagery, sections that flow into one another rather than sitting in boxes. Quiet confidence, not a sci-fi HUD.

## Anti-references
- "AI dashboard" scaffolding: boxed cards everywhere, HUD corner brackets, glowing accents, gradient text, tiny uppercase mono eyebrows over every block, numbered display codes.
- Sci-fi mission-control costume: telemetry strips, scan lines, spinning orbit rings, count-up numbers.
- Hard, boxy sharpness for its own sake (an earlier attempt read as rigid, not professional).

## Design Principles
1. Every number leads back to its NASA source; the design never adds certainty the data does not have.
2. One idea per section. Let space and scale create hierarchy, not borders and boxes.
3. Sections blend: tonal transitions and real imagery instead of panels and dividers.
4. The tools disappear into the task: familiar controls, consistent vocabulary, motion only for state.
5. Real NASA imagery and the app's own data visuals carry the visual weight, not decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA: body text ≥4.5:1 on the dark canvas, visible focus states, colour never the only signal in charts (stripes and markers already used), full reduced-motion support.
