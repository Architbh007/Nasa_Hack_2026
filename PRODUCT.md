# Product

## Register

product

## Users
NASA Space Apps 2026 judges, aerospace engineers and students looking at crewed-habitat fire safety. They see it mostly on a laptop or a projector during a 3-minute pitch, then explore on their own. The job: pick a cabin atmosphere, understand quickly whether a fire is likely and whether NASA has tested it, and trace every claim back to a real NASA source.

## Product Purpose
FireSight summarizes, ranks and interprets NASA microgravity combustion research (PSI) for Moon, Mars and ISS cabin fire safety. It shows fire risk on a simple grid, plays out a fire in a cabin (Fire Twin), explains it with a grounded SI layer, and links to the original NASA pages. Success: a judge understands the risk-and-evidence story in under a minute and trusts the numbers' provenance.

## Brand Personality
Credible, calm, mission-control. Graphite grays with a quiet sense of deep space (subtle starfield, faint nebula light), instrument-panel precision in type and data. Awe is a backdrop, never in the way of reading the data.

## Anti-references
- Generic purple/pink AI SaaS: gradient washes, glowing glass cards everywhere.
- Plain corporate admin dashboard: flat white/gray template with no personality.

## Design Principles
- Data first: the background sets the mood; charts, grid colours and text always win the contrast fight.
- Show provenance: every number links to its evidence or labels itself synthetic/illustrative.
- Projector-proof: readable from the back of a room; no low-contrast gray-on-black.
- Quiet motion: slow, purposeful movement that never competes with the Fire Twin animation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA contrast. Colour-blind-safe data palette (blue / amber / red plus stripes). All background motion stops under prefers-reduced-motion.
